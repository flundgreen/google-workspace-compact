# Privacy Policy – Workspace Compact

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

Workspace Compact (the Chrome extension and the userscripts in this repository) does **not** collect, store, transmit or sell any data.

- It runs only on `docs.google.com` (Google Docs, Sheets and Slides editors).
- It changes how the page is laid out: it moves the menu bar, resizes a few header elements, and closes the Gemini suggestion bar in Slides.
- It does not read the contents of your documents, your account details, or your browsing history.
- It makes no network requests and uses no analytics, cookies or storage.
- All of its code is included in the extension package. Nothing is downloaded at runtime.

Questions: please open an issue at https://github.com/flundgreen/google-workspace-compact/issues
